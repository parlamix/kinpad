# KinPad security

## Report privately

Email suspected vulnerabilities to [security@kinpad.org](mailto:security@kinpad.org) before public disclosure. Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected KinPad version, Windows version, observed behavior, security impact, and the smallest safe reproduction available.

Do not include manuscripts, document contents, recovery files, credentials, private keys, or other sensitive data unless they are essential and secure handling has first been agreed.

## Scope

Reports may cover the KinPad application, official installers, replacement behavior, recovery-data handling, download integrity, or KinPad product infrastructure.

Microsoft WebView2 and operating-system vulnerabilities may belong to their respective vendors. Reports that appear to affect KinPad may still be submitted for triage.

## Supported versions

Only the latest official KinPad release receives support and security fixes unless its release notes say otherwise.

KinPad is maintained by one developer. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response or repair time.

The complete policy, including good-faith research terms, is available at [kinpad.org/security](https://kinpad.org/security/).
