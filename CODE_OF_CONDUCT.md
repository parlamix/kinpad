# Code of conduct

KinPad's public spaces are for useful, respectful product discussion.

## Expected

- Be clear, patient, and relevant.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Protect personal, confidential, and security-sensitive information.
- Respect a decision after it has been explained.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Spam, repeated disruption, or deliberately misleading reports.
- Publishing private information or vulnerability details that could put people at risk.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed. Context, impact, and repeated behavior will guide moderation.

For conduct concerns, email [hello@kinpad.org](mailto:hello@kinpad.org). Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
